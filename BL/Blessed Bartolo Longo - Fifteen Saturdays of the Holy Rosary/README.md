# The Fifteen Saturdays of the Holy Rosary

*Meditations by **Blessed Bartolo Longo** (1841–1926), Apostle of the Holy Rosary and founder of the Shrine of Pompeii.*

A series of fifteen meditations, one for each Saturday, corresponding to the fifteen mysteries of the Holy Rosary: the five **Joyful**, five **Sorrowful**, and five **Glorious** Mysteries.

Each meditation includes a Gospel / Scripture reading, a numbered meditation, a virtue to practice, a reflection, and an ejaculation.
